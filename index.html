Professional Profile

My core professional focus is credit-risk modelling, credit strategy and decision analytics. I work across the full model lifecycle—from data and target design, segmentation and feature engineering through estimation, calibration, validation, monitoring and redevelopment—but also on what comes after the model: how risk estimates should translate into business decisions. This includes acceptance/rejection strategies, risk-based pricing and cut-offs, credit-limit and exposure rules, customer and portfolio segmentation, early-warning actions, portfolio growth versus risk trade-offs, and the optimisation of expected loss, utilisation and commercial outcomes.

I therefore approach credit risk as both a modelling and a decision problem. My work combines statistical and machine-learning methods with portfolio analytics, financial econometrics and practical governance, so that models are not only statistically sound but also useful for real lending decisions, monitoring, policy design and portfolio management.
